Live measurements and the combined map

Sensor IDs: boom, rear, left, right. Send each sensor at least once per second. Readings expire after 3 seconds.

{"sensor":"rear","distance_m":1.25,
 "pose":{"x_m":0,"y_m":-2.2,"yaw_deg":180}}

Poses are in meters from the vehicle origin: x = right, y = forward. Yaw rotates clockwise from forward. Supply calibrated mounting positions; update the boom pose as its arm moves. Without a pose, distance alerts work and the map labels the reading unplaced.

Send scan rays and multi-sensor frames to combine real data. See the repository sensor protocol guide for the full format.
